### 🚀 About Me

I am an **AI Engineer and Systems Developer** passionate about turning cutting-edge machine learning research into fast, scalable, real-world software. My work bridges the gap between deep learning models (clinical LLMs, medical computer vision, edge TinyML) and low-level high-performance systems engineering (CUDA, C++, GPU acceleration).

- 🔭 **Currently Building**: [**ShifoAI**](https://github.com/Zizx4uM/shifoai-uzbek-mvp) — An end-to-end medical triage & clinical dialogue assistant powered by MedGemma specialized for the Uzbek language.
- ⚡ **Deep Diving**: High-performance GPU computing & custom CUDA kernels on NVIDIA Ada Lovelace architecture.
- 🔬 **Research & Domain Work**: Medical multi-modal modeling (`MedSAM`, `BiomedCLIP`, `CheXagent`, `TorchXRayVision`, `PubMedBERT`).
- 🌾 **Edge Computing**: TinyML models for low-power agricultural diagnostics and crop disease detection.
